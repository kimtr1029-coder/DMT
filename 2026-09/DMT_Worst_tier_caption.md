**Suggested caption.** Hardware datapath for BER checking and worst-tier selection. Per-tone results accumulate the bit-weighted error E_w, loaded bits B, and four tier records (M, E_M, N_M). The global comparison generates target_met. A single 32 × 9-bit multiplier serially produces six registered cross products. Two compare/select stages retain winners A (256 vs 64) and B (16 vs 4), which return through the A/B operand MUX for the final comparison. The final compare/select stage emits j_w.

**Definitions.** E_w = Σ_k b_k p_k, B = Σ_k b_k, E_M = Σ_{k:M_k=M} p_k, and N_M is the number of tones in tier M. For nonempty tiers, E_i N_j ≥ E_j N_i compares E_i/N_i against E_j/N_j without dividers. Empty tiers are excluded. The global predicate is (B = 0) OR (10⁴ E_w ≤ G B), where G = 16,617,216 in the supplied Q24 RTL. Equal mean errors favor higher-order tiers.

**Drawing scope.** Product pairs depict six separately written 41-bit registers, not six multipliers. CMP/SEL groups a cross-product comparator, nonempty-tier qualification, and record-selection logic. The two outputs target_met and j_w feed the surrounding search controller.
