**Per-tone RTL microarchitecture.** Four Q16 base thresholds and margin words feed four 33-bit add/compare lanes. A priority encoder generates the selected tier or OFF. R1 captures that tier and the linear SNR. One K selection and a 16×32-bit multiplier produce Y; R2 captures the tier and the fields f=Y[31:27], q=Y[36:32], and s=(Y≥24). The 32-entry LUT, one C selection and a 16×17-bit multiplier produce the Q32 product. The [31:8] slice is shifted by q and suppressed by s or OFF before R3 captures the Q24 BER and tier.
