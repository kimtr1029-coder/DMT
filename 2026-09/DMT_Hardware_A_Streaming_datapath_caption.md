**Suggested caption.** Streaming tone selection and BER datapath. Four Q16 margin-adjusted thresholds are compared in parallel; a priority encoder chooses the QAM tier. The three-register pipeline selects tier coefficients, forms the fixed-point exponent and table address, then produces Q24 BER. Independent feedback accumulators collect total bits B and bit-weighted error E, with four per-tier error/count records. The search hardware in the companion figure supplies margin writes and consumes scan statistics.
