**Suggested paper caption.** Per-tone QAM selection and BER-estimation datapath. Four margin-adjusted SNR comparators drive a priority encoder; R1 registers the highest qualified QAM tier and linear SNR. The tier selects Q16 coefficients K and C. A 16 × 32-bit multiplication forms Q32 Y, and R2 captures its fractional LUT address f, integer shift q, saturation flag s, and aligned tier. The 32-word LUT and a 16 × 17-bit multiplication produce a Q24 value, which is shifted by q and forced to zero by s before R3 captures the BER estimate and tier.

**Technical notes.** QAM tiers are selected in the order 256, 64, 16, 4; OFF is selected if all four threshold comparisons fail. The four entries in each K/C bank show tier indexing, not numeric coefficient values. The 4 × 8 LUT cells represent 32 addressed words; the pink cell is an illustrative selection. Define real y = Y/2^32. R2 records f = Y[31:27], q = Y[36:32], and s = (y ≥ 24). The LUT contains 17-bit Q16 approximations of 2^(−f/32). After multiplication by Q16 C, slice [31:8] is a Q24 value. An OFF tier produces a zero estimate. Clock, reset, valid, control implementation and upstream SNR conversion are omitted.
